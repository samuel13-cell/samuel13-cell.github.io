India's electric car problem

The share of new cars sold in India that are electric nearly doubled in 2025. It is still 4.0%, against a world average of 25%. Both of those facts are true and most coverage picks one.

Growth rates flatter small numbers. Doubling from 2.1% to 4.0% is a real acceleration, and it is also the kind of move that is easy to make from a low base. The level matters more than the rate here: on share of new sales, India ranks 57 of 61 countries reporting a 2025 figure.

India against the markets it is usually compared to

Where the 2025 figures sit

The countries at the top are small, wealthy and cold, with dense charging networks and, in most cases, years of purchase subsidies behind them. That is the honest reason for the gap: India's car market is price-led in a way Norway's is not, and an electric car still carries a purchase premium that most Indian buyers will not pay without a subsidy.

Method

Our World in Data publishes the share of new car sales that are electric, compiled from the IEA's Global EV Outlook, as a single CSV covering 2010 to 2025. Electric here means battery electric and plug-in hybrid together, which is the IEA's definition.

The ranking counts only entities carrying a country code, which excludes aggregates such as the world average and regional groupings. Countries that did not report a 2025 figure are absent from the ranking rather than counted as zero.

What this does not show

This is new cars only. It says nothing about two and three wheelers, which is where India's electrification is actually happening at scale, nor about buses. A country can look slow on this measure while moving quickly on the vehicles its people mostly buy, and India is the clearest example of exactly that.

It is also a share, not a count. India's car market is large enough that 4.0% of it is a substantial number of vehicles, and a high share of a small market can be fewer cars than a low share of a big one.

Source: Our World in Data, from the IEA Global EV Outlook, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/electric-car-sales-share.csv
